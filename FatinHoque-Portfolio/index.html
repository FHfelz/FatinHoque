Projects

32 bit MIPS processor

A 32 bit single cycle processor in SystemVerilog, implemented on a ZedBoard. I used the board switches to select registers and read their values through the LEDs, one byte at a time.

One bug came down to a single instruction word: I had encoded a beq as 01520001 instead of 11520001. The PC took the wrong path and later register checks failed. Correcting the encoding fixed the testbench.

CMOS common source amplifier

The assignment gave us three targets: gain above 20 dB, unity gain frequency above 200 MHz, and power below 1 mW. My design simulated at 20.62 dB, 262.3 MHz, and 3.3 μW.

I used PMOS current mirror biasing and completed the schematic and layout in Cadence Virtuoso. The gain cleared the requirement by a small margin; the power was well below budget.

Maze navigating robot

Our robot initially made it through about half its attempts. I wrote the C++ firmware and helped change the sensor layout and turning logic. With five front sensors and count based turns, it finished 26 of 28 trials and passed the live demo.
